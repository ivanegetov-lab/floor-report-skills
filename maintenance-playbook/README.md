## What's inside

- **Part 1 — Why This Playbook Exists.** For the maintenance manager who sees the problems but can't always get the budget to fix them. A field guide, not a software pitch.
- **Part 2 — Foundations.** The work management cycle: *identify → plan → schedule → execute → close out and learn.* No KPI means anything if the underlying cycle is broken. Start here if you are not sure where to start.
- **Part 3 — Where Are You.** A self-assessment maturity model with a benchmark snapshot. Tells you honestly where you sit — not where last year's audit said you sat.
- **Part 4 — The Ten Playbooks.** Ten specific plays for ten specific KPI failures. Each has a ninety-day roadmap. Pick the one that matches your worst number right now.
- **Part 5 — Tools and Cadences.** RCA quick guide, ninety-day implementation sequence, full reference list.

## Who it's for

The maintenance manager at a mid-size plant who already knows what's broken. The gearbox that failed in January, August, and October. The PMs that keep slipping. The backlog that keeps growing. You don't need another diagram of the reliability pyramid — you need a way to get the next thing fixed before it becomes the thing that shows up on Monday morning for the fourth time.
